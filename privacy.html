Privacy Policy

LongPrairiePlumbing.com | Independent plumbing information and telephone enquiries

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used and shared when you visit LongPrairiePlumbing.com or telephone the number displayed here. This is an independently operated plumbing information and lead-generation website. We do not carry out plumbing repairs ourselves.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, information may include your telephone number and caller ID, the date and time of your call, call duration, voicemail or other recorded call content, and details you voluntarily provide about a plumbing problem, your location or how to contact you. Our hosting and telecommunications systems may also process basic technical information needed to operate the website and handle enquiries. This page does not currently offer a contact form.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to receive and respond to enquiries, understand the nature and quality of incoming calls, identify potential service needs, route or refer enquiries to relevant independent plumbing providers, maintain and improve the website and calling arrangements, investigate misuse, and meet legal obligations.

Lead Generation and Independent Providers

This website is intended to generate plumbing enquiries. When you call, your enquiry may be handled by the website operator or transferred or referred to an independent third-party business offering plumbing services. We may receive compensation for qualifying calls or referrals. Independent providers determine their own services, availability, prices and practices and may collect and use information under their own privacy policies. Calling us does not, by itself, authorise unrelated marketing calls or texts.

Call Tracking, Voicemail and Recording

We use telecommunications services to route and measure calls. Incoming calls may be tracked and recorded, including voicemail messages left by callers, for enquiry assessment, verification, quality and administration. If a call is connected to a third-party provider, call information may be transferred to that provider and recording arrangements may differ. Where applicable law requires notice or consent for recording, appropriate notice or consent must be provided. This policy is not a substitute for any notice or consent required when a call takes place.

Cookies and Website Analytics

The website may receive basic technical information necessary for hosting and operation. We do not currently identify any separate advertising pixel or analytics service in the website code. If additional cookies or analytics are introduced, this policy should be updated to explain their use and any required choices.

Information Sharing

We may share relevant enquiry information with independent service providers so they can assess or respond to a requested plumbing enquiry. Information may also be processed by hosting and telecommunications providers who help operate the website or its telephone facilities. We may disclose information where necessary to comply with law or protect against misuse. We do not promise that enquiry information will remain solely with the website operator.

Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the services used to protect information. No internet or telecommunications system can be guaranteed completely secure. Call records and enquiry information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed for operational, verification, legal or dispute-resolution purposes, then deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements. We do not specify a fixed retention period that has not been established.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the laws applicable to you and the information concerned, you may be able to ask about personal information held by the website operator, request a correction or deletion, or exercise other applicable privacy rights. Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify a request and may not be able to control records independently held by third-party providers.

Third-Party Websites and Services

Independent plumbing providers, telephone providers and external websites may maintain separate privacy notices and policies. Please review their information practices before giving them additional personal details.

Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy when the website, telephone handling arrangements or relevant requirements change. The current version will appear on this page.
